## Post 3
Trace's data model is intentionally not Contact/Deal/Stage. It's Person → Event → Signal → Timeline.

Three baseline signals every cycle:
・high_intent — multiple touchpoints in 7 days
・power_user — sustained issue/PR activity
・feature_advocate — repeated 👍 on the same proposal
